# BLACK 0.10.0: choose the aiming mark's shape

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Added

- **Reticle Shape.** A Mods row changes the aiming mark: the game's own dot
  (the default), a ring, a ring with ticks, a cross or chevrons. Every shape
  comes from the game's own HUD texture, so no picture is shipped, and it
  works with the Original and HD Reticle textures. **Auto** picks one by the
  weapon in your hands: a ring for the shotguns and launchers, the game's own
  mark for the sniper rifle, a cross for the other weapons. The game still
  colours the mark; the sniper scope, the medkit cross and the damage marks
  at the frame's edges are unchanged. Off by default.

Everything from 0.9.0 remains. Settings, saves and checkpoints carry over.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.10.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place. When the game first reads your profile, BLACK backs it up if it changed since the last backup.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, in bounded first-mission runs from the
developer's own saves: the ring and the chevrons with HD Reticle, the ring
with ticks and the cross with the Original texture, each at the centre in the
game's neutral colour; with Auto the pistol showed the cross and the sniper
rifle its own mark, and from a mission-2 checkpoint the RPG showed the ring
and the AK the cross. **128 CTest checks** and **49 Python tests** passed on
the release build. The final DMG was checked read-only in English and Russian
with isolated data and system-only PATH: both opened the settings and quit
normally, and source game files and user preferences kept their hashes. The
matching source archive was verified without game or private data. Not
checked: a shotgun or the grenade launcher in play, the ally, enemy and object
colours on the new shapes, switching the row from the open menu during play,
Steam Deck.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.10.0/docs/VALIDATION.md).
