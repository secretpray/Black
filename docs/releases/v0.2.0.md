# BLACK 0.2.0 — Mods and FPS counter

An experimental native macOS release of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple Silicon.
Bring your own supported USA or European disc image. No game assets are included.

## What's new

- **Mods page.** The settings panel gains a fourth page, laid out like TGP's menu:
  - **Films:** Skip Intro Movies and Skip Mission Intros, moved here from Recomp Settings.
  - **Player:** God Mode, Infinite Medkits, Infinite Grenades, Infinite Ammo through
    the game's own Silver Weapons reward, and Always Silenced (experimental).
  - **Unlocks:** all missions, the Black Ops difficulty, the M16A2 at mission start
    and the BFG code.

  Every mod is off by default. Infinite Ammo and the M16A2 take effect from the next
  mission start or restart. Unlocks are saved into your profile, and turning a mod
  off never relocks earned progress. Addresses are kept per XBE revision; an
  unrecognised executable shows **Mods off** and nothing is changed.
- **FPS Counter** in Recomp Settings → Display: a small corner label showing the
  game's own frame rate. BLACK runs near 30 frames per second by design.

## Install or update

1. Download **`BLACK-0.2.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**.
2. Drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**, replacing the old app, then open it.
3. On first launch, choose your own Original Xbox BLACK ISO/XISO or extracted disc
   folder. An existing installation, settings and saves are kept.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc signed,
not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the download source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

Data lives in `~/Library/Application Support/BLACK/`. Your 0.1.0 settings carry over;
the new mod keys start off.

**Settings:** F1/F10, Cmd+, or Back + Start; Q/E or LB/RB change page.
**Fullscreen:** Cmd+Return. **Quit:** Cmd+Q.
[Quick start, screenshots and controls](https://github.com/secretpray/Black#quick-start).

## Tested

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, first mission on Normal with the player mods on:
the game reported the USA revision, health stayed at its 1200 maximum, medkits at
three and grenades at nine, and the HUD showed 999 rounds and the M16A2.
101 CTest checks, 30 Python checks and the scripted settings-menu check passed.
The packaged app, run from its mounted DMG with a system-only PATH, enabled the
DSP56300 JIT, reported the USA revision and quit normally before its time limit.

**Limits:** input was scripted, not a physical controller. Damage above the maximum
in a single frame can still kill in God Mode. Always Silenced and the BFG were not
exercised in combat. Texture replacement is not available on macOS yet; the PCSX2 HD
texture pack for the PS2 version does not apply to this game. A complete playthrough,
stable 60 FPS, Intel Macs, Linux and Steam Deck are not qualified.
[Validation and limits](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/Black/docs/VALIDATION.md).
