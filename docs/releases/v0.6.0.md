# BLACK 0.6.0: the game's brightness, Home button and film skipping

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## New

- **The game's own brightness.** BLACK builds a gamma ramp for the Xbox's video
  output: its brightness curve, the brightness slider in its options and its
  fades to and from black. On the Mac that ramp never reached the screen, so the
  picture was duller and the fades were missing. The presenter now applies it
  as the last step, as the Xbox's video output does.
- **Home / Xbox Guide / PS button.** A tap opens or closes the settings. Hold it
  for 1.5 seconds for a quit page in the style of the game's pause menu:
  **Continue**, **Quit to Main Menu** (in a mission: the game's pause menu and
  its own confirmation, so checkpoint progress is lost only after YES there) and
  **Quit Game**. While BLACK has focus, macOS gives the button to the game
  instead of Game Center and hands it back when you switch away.
- **Quit Game** in the settings footer and on Port Settings opens the same page.
- **Skip Films with a Button** (Mods → Films, on by default): A, Start or Enter
  skips any film, also the logos and films the game only lets you skip after
  that mission. A hint shows the button after 2.5 seconds; looping menu
  backgrounds stay. The automatic options are renamed **Auto-Skip Intro Films**
  and **Auto-Skip Mission Briefings**; your choices are kept.

## Fixed

- **A grid flickering in blur and glow** at render scales above 1×, most visible
  on reloads against a bright background. The game's small glow and blur buffers
  are now averaged over one Xbox texel before its blur samples them.
- **The Dock.** A click on the Dock icon brings BLACK back, and Quit in the Dock
  menu, log out and restart close it normally instead of being ignored.
- **PRESS START** keeps the game's own words in its language instead of a
  button icon that read as OPTIONS on a DualSense.

Everything from 0.5.5 and earlier remains: steady frame rate in later areas,
bloom and sun shafts, the title screen text, HD texture alpha, controller
hotplug and the HD pack check. No settings or save migration is required.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.6.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1** the user compared the brightness by eye in
the menus and missions 1 and 8 ("very close to the original on the Xbox"), moved
the brightness slider to both ends, saw the fades on loading and death, skipped
films with the hint on a DualSense, no longer saw the blur grid on reloads, and
used Home with Game Center staying closed. With the release app the user
brought the game back with a Dock click and closed it from the Dock menu. A
scripted mission run took Quit to Main Menu through the game's pause menu to
its own confirmation. **115 CTest
checks** and **49 Python tests** passed. The final DMG was checked read-only in
English and Russian with isolated data and system-only PATH: both opened
settings and quit normally, and source game files and user preferences kept
their hashes. The matching source archive was verified without game or private
data.

Not checked: the Home button on controllers other than the DualSense, a Dock
click on a minimized window, log out or restart with the game open (they send
the same quit event as the Dock menu), and a full playthrough with the new
brightness. Other
limits of 0.5.5 still apply.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.6.0/docs/VALIDATION.md).
