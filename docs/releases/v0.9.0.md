# BLACK 0.9.0: HD Reticle, frame times and a sniper in the first mission

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Added

- **HD Reticle.** General → HD Reticle rebuilds the game's own HUD texture at
  four times its size: a sharper aiming dot and crisper small HUD marks, such
  as the damage marks at the frame's edges. It is made from your game's own
  texture, so no picture is shipped. The game still colours the dot: white,
  green on an ally, red on an enemy, black on a destructible object. It works
  with or without the HD texture pack. Off by default; changes live.
- **Performance Overlay.** The FPS Counter row becomes Performance Overlay:
  off, the game's frame rate with the display's beside it, or frame times as
  well (the median, 95th percentile and worst frame of the last moments). A
  saved FPS Counter setting keeps its meaning.
- **Black Box.** BLACK keeps the last minute of controller input the game
  actually saw. Controls → Gamepad and Vibration → Advanced Gamepad →
  Controller Diagnostics → **Save Black Box** writes it beside the settings as
  a CSV file headed by a copy of your settings, to attach to a report about
  the controls.
- **Sniper in the First Mission.** A Mods row puts the sniper rifle in your
  second slot when the first mission starts, with five magazines. The
  mission's own first weapon stays, and its scope and ammunition are the
  game's own. Off by default.

## Changed

- **Lower CPU use.** BLACK's graphics thread now waits instead of spinning:
  in a mission the app used about 30-56 % of a core's time instead of about
  116 %, at the same 30 frames per second.
- **Settings.** A long path at the top of a sub-page folds its first steps
  instead of running under the controller status; on Controller Diagnostics
  the values no longer overlap the stick monitor.

Everything from 0.8.0 remains. Settings, saves and checkpoints carry over.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.9.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place. When the game first reads your profile, BLACK backs it up if it changed since the last backup.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, in bounded mission runs from the
developer's own saves: HD Reticle at render scales 2 and 4, with SMAA and FXAA
and with the HD pack, kept 30 frames per second against the original texture,
and the developer saw the dot's four colours and switched it live (before
the texture became shared; the live switch was not repeated after); a fresh
first mission gave the sniper, and Continue Mission from a checkpoint kept the
checkpoint's weapons; the overlay and the Black Box were checked in the
settings and in missions, and a saved Black Box played back reached the same
checkpoint. **127 CTest checks** and **49 Python tests** passed. Not checked:
the rings and crosses that share the HUD texture, seen in motion; the pause
menu's Restart and F9 with the sniper; Steam Deck.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.9.0/docs/VALIDATION.md).
