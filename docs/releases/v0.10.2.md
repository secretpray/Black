# BLACK 0.10.2: one aiming cross, made for aiming

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Changed

- **Reticle Shape's cross is redrawn.** Four short bars around a small open
  centre, so the target under the mark stays visible, with a dark rim that
  keeps it readable on bright walls, smoke and sky. It is about the size of the
  game's dot: 1.7% of the screen's height across, two-pixel bars at 1080p.
  The game still colours it (green on an ally, red on an enemy, black on a
  destructible object), and **Auto** gives it to every weapon but the
  shotguns, launchers and the sniper rifle.

## Removed

- **Chevron Up and Crosshair** from 0.10.1. BLACK never moves or grows its
  aiming mark, and a standing shot lands within about half a percent of the
  screen's height of it, so a crosshair gap the size of Call of Duty's showed
  a spread two to three times the game's, and the small chevron's aim point
  read below its tip. A mark that misleads the aim is worse than none.

## Settings

Reticle Shape's values are 0.10.0's again (Dot, Ring, Ring and Ticks, Cross,
Chevrons, Auto). A 0.10.1 setting is read once: Chevron Up becomes the dot,
Crosshair the cross, and Auto stays Auto. Everything else from 0.10.1
remains. Settings, saves and checkpoints carry over.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.10.2-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place. When the game first reads your profile, BLACK backs it up if it changed since the last backup.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**: a reticle trace over every logged run saw
the game's mark only at the centre and at one size; a per-frame dump of the
player's weapon while standing, firing and walking found no value that grows
with firing like a bloom; ten standing pistol shots landed within about 0.5% of
the height of the dot. In bounded first-mission runs with HD Reticle (from a
0.10.1 Crosshair setting) and with the Original texture, the new cross was
drawn at the centre with its open centre about 0.65% of the height across and
read on a dark wall, bright smoke and over a soldier. With the Original texture
its vertical bars are a little dimmer than the horizontal ones.
**128 CTest checks** and **49 Python tests** passed on the release build. The
final DMG was checked read-only in English and Russian with isolated data and
system-only PATH: both opened the settings and quit normally, and source game
files and user preferences kept their hashes. The matching source archive was
verified without game or private data. Not checked: Steam Deck.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.10.2/docs/VALIDATION.md).
