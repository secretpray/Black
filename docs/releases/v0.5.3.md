# BLACK 0.5.3: HD texture alpha fix

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Fixed

- **Black grenade crate and white weapons with HD textures.** The PCSX2 pack
  keeps PS2 alpha, which for some textures means something else than on Xbox.
  In the first mission it painted the grenade crate black with white grenades
  and weapons lying on the ground pale white. BLACK now compares the two alpha
  masks and keeps the game's own texture where they disagree. The rifle in hand,
  ammunition and most effects keep their HD files.

Everything from 0.5.2 remains: controller hotplug over USB and Bluetooth, a
Bluetooth DualSense that answers sooner after the desktop, and the HD pack check
with folder selection. No settings or save migration is required.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.5.3-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1** the user confirmed the grenade crate and a
rifle on the ground in the first mission after the fix; five first-mission
textures fall back to the game's own. **114 CTest checks** and **49 Python tests**
passed. The final DMG was checked read-only in English and
Russian with isolated data and system-only PATH: both opened settings and quit
normally, and source game files and user preferences kept their hashes. The
matching source archive was verified without game or private data.

Missions 2–8 have not been checked for the same mismatch by eye. The M16 held
in the first mission looks grey with and without HD; that rendering is a
separate open issue.
Other limits of 0.5.2 still apply.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.5.3/docs/VALIDATION.md).
