# BLACK 0.4.0 — first-time setup, Russian and the game's own look

An experimental native macOS release of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple Silicon.
Bring your own supported USA or European disc image. No game assets are included.

## What's new

- **First-time setup** drawn like BLACK's own menu pages: the rails, the heading
  typed in with a block cursor, green-grey rows and the selector bar, and the game's
  fonts read from your disc as soon as it is checked.
  - Choose your ISO/XISO or extracted disc folder, or drop it onto the window.
  - Before anything is copied you see the release, the game text languages, the size
    and the free space on your Mac.
  - The copy shows progress, speed, time left and the current file. **Stop** keeps
    what was copied; the next install, or the next launch, carries on from there.
  - Then pick the game text and the settings panel's language, and the graphics:
    render scale, anti-aliasing, MetalFX upscaling, texture filtering, field of view,
    or **Use Recommended**. Each choice is saved at once and applies on the first start.
- **Russian game text.** The app carries only the translation text. Choose Russian
  in setup or in Recomp Settings → General: before the game starts, the app builds the
  Russian string bank from your own disc's English bank and adds the Cyrillic letters
  to your own copy of the game's fonts. Nothing to do by hand, and every other
  language keeps its text. Credits names, trademarks and web addresses stay as on
  the disc.
- **Recomp settings in the game's look:** the gold palette carried over from TGP's
  other ports is replaced by BLACK's own menu colours and rails. Layout and controls
  are unchanged.
- **German, Spanish and Italian** game text checked from the menus into the first
  mission, with every accented letter in the game's fonts.

## Install or update

1. Download **`BLACK-0.4.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**.
2. Drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**, replacing the old app, then open it.
3. A new install starts the first-time setup. An existing installation, settings and
   saves are kept and the setup does not appear; Russian is offered in Recomp Settings
   → General → Game Language and applies from the next launch.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc signed,
not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the download source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

Data lives in `~/Library/Application Support/BLACK/`. Choosing Russian adds
`MainEn.bin`, `MainRu.bin` and the fonts' retail copies (`*.retail.bin`) to your
installed game there; your disc image is never changed.

**Settings:** F1/F10, Cmd+, or Back + Start; Q/E or LB/RB change page.
**Fullscreen:** Cmd+Return. **Quit:** Cmd+Q.
[Quick start, screenshots and controls](https://github.com/secretpray/Black#quick-start).

## Tested

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**:

- Setup with the European folder, the European ISO and the USA ISO: each checked,
  stopped at a third of the copy with the copied files kept, then resumed through the
  setup and verified. Both ISOs were unchanged afterwards. The real app with an empty
  data folder went from setup into the game and quit normally; a person installed the
  European ISO through the window and played.
- Russian through the app on the European folder and the USA ISO: the main menu,
  difficulty choice, briefing, mission objectives and pause menu in Russian; switching
  back to English restored the English bank without touching the fonts again.
- German, Spanish and Italian from the main menu into the first mission and its pause
  menu, with the game's own string banks.
- The packaged app from its DMG: a new install with English left the game's fonts as
  they were; switching to Russian built it on the next start.
- 106 CTest checks, 43 Python checks, the settings-menu preview and the setup preview
  passed.

**Limits:** the setup has been used with mouse and keyboard; controller navigation and
dropping a file were not tested by hand. Russian and the other languages were checked
in the first mission only; film subtitles are not included. The 12–16 FPS slowdown
once seen in a later fourth-mission area with HD textures at 2048 did not recur, and
its cause is not established. A complete playthrough, stable 60 FPS, Intel Macs,
Linux and Steam Deck are not qualified.
[Validation and limits](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/Black/docs/VALIDATION.md).

## Downloads

- **`BLACK-0.4.0-macos-arm64.dmg`** — the app.
- **`optional-library-sources.tar.xz`** — exact runtime/game-host/DSP sources, local
  overlays, library source archives and build/relink recipes.
- **`SHA256SUMS`** — download integrity checks.

No ISO, original XBE, game assets, generated game C, HD texture pack, private saves or
session logs are included. You must supply your own supported game disc.
