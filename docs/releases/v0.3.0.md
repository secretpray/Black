# BLACK 0.3.0 — HD textures, languages and more mods

An experimental native macOS release of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple Silicon.
Bring your own supported USA or European disc image. No game assets are included.

## What's new

- **HD texture replacement** (Recomp Settings → Textures, off by default). Uses your own
  copy of the PCSX2 "BLACK HD Reimagined" pack, through portingpete's MIT-licensed map
  of Xbox textures to pack files. Put the pack's files, as they are, under
  `~/Library/Application Support/BLACK/Textures/replacement/`. Files load in the
  background and a missing one keeps the game's own texture. **HD Texture Size**
  (1024, 2048 or 4096, default 2048) caps the largest side to save memory. The pack is
  not included.
- **Game Language** (Recomp Settings → General): English, French, German, Spanish or
  Italian game text from your own disc's string banks, from the next launch. The
  European disc has all five; the USA disc has English only.
- **Menu Language** for the settings panel: Auto, English, French, German, Spanish,
  Italian or Russian.
- **More mods**, ported from TGP482/BLACK-XBERecomp PR 141:
  - **Infinite Ammo:** your magazine and reserve never go down, with any weapon you hold.
  - **Rapid Fire:** 2X, 3X or 4X, at most one shot a frame.
  - **No Recoil:** your shots no longer kick the view up.
  - **One-Hit Kill:** any hit of your bullets kills a soldier.
  - **Game Speed:** 50%, 75%, 150% or 200% of the pace of play; menus and films keep
    theirs.
  - **God Mode** now also holds the game's own invulnerability flag, so a single huge
    hit no longer kills.
  - Two renames: "Infinite Ammo (Silver Weapons)" is now **Silver Weapons**, and the
    "BFG (M249) Code" is **M249 in the First Mission**. That is all it does: the
    developers' code gives an M249 with 600 rounds in the first mission only, and the
    gun has no texture there.
- **Fixes:** a malformed texture mode no longer aborts the game (a long fourth-mission
  session had ended with SIGABRT), and quitting no longer clicks in the speakers.

## Install or update

1. Download **`BLACK-0.3.0-macos-arm64.dmg`**.
2. Drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**, replacing the old app, then open it.
3. On first launch, choose your own Original Xbox BLACK ISO/XISO or extracted disc
   folder. An existing installation, settings and saves are kept.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc signed,
not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the download source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

Data lives in `~/Library/Application Support/BLACK/`. Your 0.2.0 settings and mods
carry over; the new options start off.

**Settings:** F1/F10, Cmd+, or Back + Start; Q/E or LB/RB change page.
**Fullscreen:** Cmd+Return. **Quit:** Cmd+Q.
[Quick start, screenshots and controls](https://github.com/secretpray/Black#quick-start).

## Tested

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, mostly in the first mission on Normal:

- Each new mod was checked in play: 90 shots without a reload with Infinite Ammo, a
  shot every 3 frames instead of 10 with Rapid Fire 4X, no view kick with No Recoil,
  one shot per kill on eight soldiers with One-Hit Kill (played by hand), the
  player's own grenade taking no health with God Mode, and Game Speed firing and
  walking at half and double pace at a steady 30 FPS.
- HD textures in the first mission: 115 replacements, no failed files, 381 MB
  resident, 30 FPS. In the fourth mission at render scale 2: 30.0 FPS without HD,
  30.0 at 1024 and 29.7 at 2048.
- French game text in the front end; the Russian and Spanish settings pages.
- 105 CTest checks, 39 Python checks and the scripted settings-menu check passed.

**Limits:** input was scripted except where noted, not a physical controller. HD
textures cover only part of each level (50 of 94 unique textures in a second-mission
audit); the rest keep the game's own. A long session with HD at 2048 slowed to
12–16 FPS in a later fourth-mission area; the cause is not established. Only the
front end was checked in other languages. A complete playthrough, stable 60 FPS,
Intel Macs, Linux and Steam Deck are not qualified.
[Validation and limits](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/Black/docs/VALIDATION.md).

session logs are included. You must supply your own supported game disc.
