# BLACK 0.5.2: controller hotplug, Bluetooth idle and HD pack check

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Fixed

- **Unplugging and reconnecting controllers.** USB and Bluetooth pads can be
  disconnected and reconnected during play, in menus and while rebinding.
  Held movement, fire and aim stop; keyboard/mouse take over under Auto; the
  pad returns with its bindings and prompts. Vibration stops on disconnect and
  works again from the first shot after reconnecting. Switching a DualSense
  between USB and Bluetooth, and a second DualSense, were checked too.
- **Bluetooth DualSense after the desktop.** After about five minutes away from
  a fullscreen game, the first input took about four seconds when vibration was
  off. The pad now keeps reporting while idle; the same return took about two
  seconds.
- **HD textures that seemed to do nothing.** Turning on Texture Replacement
  without a pack used to show ON and change nothing. BLACK now checks the pack,
  offers to choose its folder, explains why a folder is not usable and links a
  valid one without copying.

The Controls menu, button prompts, Russian text/subtitles and setup from earlier
releases remain included. No settings or save migration is required.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.5.2-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place. First-time users supply their own supported disc through setup.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, the user physically checked USB and
Bluetooth disconnect/reconnect with one DualSense, both transport switches,
two DualSense controllers at once, disconnecting during calibration, and the
return from the desktop with a Bluetooth DualSense. The texture pack
dialogs were checked live in Russian: no pack, a folder without textures, a
folder of unrelated pictures and the real pack, which was linked and used
without restarting.

**114 CTest checks**, GPU readback/colour probes and **49 Python tests** passed.
The final DMG was checked read-only in English and Russian with isolated data,
system-only PATH and no DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH; both runs opened settings and quit
normally before their limits, and source game files and user preferences kept
their hashes. The matching source archive was verified without game or private
data.

**Open:** about two seconds remain after a fullscreen return; a complete loss of
input after the desktop was reported once and not reproduced. Other controller
models, choosing which of several pads plays, analog trigger rebinding, a
complete playthrough, all saves/races, audio quality and a clean second Mac
remain open. Intel Macs, Linux and physical Steam Deck are not qualified.
