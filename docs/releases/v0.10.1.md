# BLACK 0.10.1: aiming marks at the dot's size and an open crosshair

An experimental native macOS update of **BLACK for Original Xbox** for Apple
Silicon. Bring your own supported USA or European disc image; no game data is
included.

## Added

- **Reticle Shape: Chevron Up and Crosshair.** **Chevron Up** is a single
  upward tick with its tip on the aim point. **Crosshair** is four short bars
  around an open centre, in the proportions of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare's
  default hip-fire crosshair: about 3.6% of the screen's height across,
  two-pixel bars at 1080p, with a faint dark rim so it reads on bright walls.

## Changed

- **The cross is small and thin.** It and Chevron Up are drawn at the size of
  the game's own dot, the cross about 1.7 dots across, instead of filling a
  larger quad.

None of the three exists in the game's HUD texture: they are painted into
three empty blocks of that atlas when its texture is built, with HD Reticle on
or off. The game still colours the mark (white, green on an ally, red on an
enemy, black on a destructible object); the medkit cross and every other HUD
sprite are unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Auto survives the update.** The new shapes move Auto to the end of the
  row, so the setting is now saved as `mod_reticle`; a 0.10.0 setting is still
  read and its Auto stays Auto.

Everything from 0.10.0 remains. Settings, saves and checkpoints carry over.

## Install or update

Download **`BLACK-0.10.1-macos-arm64.dmg`**, drag **BLACK.app** to **Applications**
and replace the old app. Existing Library game data, settings and saves remain
in place. When the game first reads your profile, BLACK backs it up if it changed since the last backup.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon and **macOS 27 or later**. The app is **ad-hoc
signed, not notarized**. If macOS blocks it, check the source and use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Validation and limits

On **Apple M1 Pro / macOS 27.0.1**, in bounded first-mission runs with HD
Reticle and with the Original texture: the thin cross, Chevron Up and Crosshair
at the centre, measured at 1.3%, 1.0% and 2.7% of the frame's height; the
Crosshair red on an enemy with its dark rim kept; Auto chose the cross for the
AK and the ring for the shotgun; the shape switched live from the settings
during play. A 0.10.0 setting with Auto is read as Auto. At render scale 2 the
Crosshair's vertical bars read slightly softer than its horizontal ones.
**128 CTest checks** and **49 Python tests** passed on the release build. The
final DMG was checked read-only in English and Russian with isolated data and
system-only PATH: both opened the settings and quit normally, and source game
files and user preferences kept their hashes. The matching source archive was
verified without game or private data. Not checked: Steam Deck.
[Validation](https://github.com/secretpray/Black/blob/v0.10.1/docs/VALIDATION.md).
